# REQ-001 change statement

The application will provide a public, demonstration-only questionnaire with
four required fields: nickname, organization, profession, and job title. Every
entry must use a `DEMO-REQ-001-<run-id>` prefix. A single Business App Owner
can view and download the resulting demonstration records as CSV.

## This change does not

It does not collect real information, offer Word export or configurable fields,
support multiple administrators, or allow visitor edits and deletions.

**Confirmed by:** Business App Owner, 2026-09-18
